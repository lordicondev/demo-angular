Icons that follow Angular state

Signals hold the state, and a binding puts it on the page. The icon reads its attributes, or the attributes of the element around it, and keeps in step. No effects, no lifecycle hooks.

Bound from a signal

[stroke] and [colors] set the icon's properties, as for any element, here from a form's model. Change them, then hover the icon.

A process, in stages

follow(data-state, busy=loop-cycle, done=morph-check) gives each value of [attr.data-state] its animation: a loop while busy, a check when done, and back.

Played from code, after a request

viewChild gives the element, with play(). The form waits for a service to answer, and the icon plays when it has.
