Loading icons when they are needed

An icon loads as soon as Angular renders it. Its attributes, and Angular's @defer, can put that off until it is needed, with something in its place meanwhile.

A placeholder, until the icon is ready

Children of <lord-icon> show until the icon is ready: here a still of each icon, a plain <img>. Slow the network down in DevTools and reload to see them.

Loading on view, or on interaction

loading="lazy" waits until the icon is in view; loading="interaction" until the pointer or the keyboard reaches it, with the placeholder on show until then. Hover the second one.

In a part that comes later

@defer (on viewport) loads this part's code once it scrolls into view, and httpResource its data. Its icons need nothing more: they load as soon as Angular renders them, and intro brings them in.
