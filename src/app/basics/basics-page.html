Animated icons in an Angular app

<lord-icon> goes straight into any template, like this page's. Its attributes say what the icon is and when it plays. Hover, click or scroll.

Triggers

Looks

The last icon takes its colour from CSS, as on a dark theme: --lord-icon-primary, in styles.css.

In buttons and links

target plays the icon when the pointer is anywhere on the button, and current-color gives it the colour of the text.
